# Addition of numbers
Adding two integers means combining their values

##Example: adding Two Positive Integers
**3** and **5**.

### Step-by-step
1. start at **3** on the number line.
2. move **5 steps to the right**.
3. You land on **8**.
3+5=8

##Example: adding a negative number and a positive Integers
**-3** and **5**.
### Step-by-step
1. start at **-3** on the number line.
2. move **5 steps to the right**.
3. You land on **2**.
-3+5=2
